Dataset attribute description (Data to be provided in Canvas):

1. PatientID: Unique identifier for each patient [3058–5162]

2. Age: Age of the patient [50–90 years]

3. Gender: Gender of the patient [0: Male, 1: Female]

4. Ethnicity: Ethnic background [0: Caucasian, 1: African American, 2: Asian, 3: Other]

5. EducationLevel: Highest education attained [0: None, 1: High School, 2: Bachelor's, 3: Higher]

6. BMI: Body Mass Index [15–40]

7. Smoking: Smoking status [0: No, 1: Yes]

8. AlcoholConsumption: Weekly alcohol consumption [0–20 units]

9. PhysicalActivity: Weekly physical activity [0–10 hours]

10. DietQuality: Diet quality score [0–10]

11. SleepQuality: Sleep quality score [4–10]

12. FamilyHistoryParkinsons: Family history of Parkinson's Disease [0: No, 1: Yes]

13. TraumaticBrainInjury: History of traumatic brain injury [0: No, 1: Yes]

14. Hypertension: Presence of hypertension [0: No, 1: Yes]

15. Diabetes: Presence of diabetes [0: No, 1: Yes]

16. Depression: Presence of depression [0: No, 1: Yes]

17. Stroke: History of stroke [0: No, 1: Yes]

18. SystolicBP: Systolic blood pressure [90–180 mmHg]

19. DiastolicBP: Diastolic blood pressure [60–120 mmHg]

20. CholesterolTotal: Total cholesterol [150–300 mg/dL]

21. CholesterolLDL: LDL cholesterol [50–200 mg/dL]

22. CholesterolHDL: HDL cholesterol [20–100 mg/dL]

23. CholesterolTriglycerides: Triglyceride levels [50–400 mg/dL]

24. UPDRS: Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale score [0–199] — higher scores indicate greater disease severity

25. MoCA: Montreal Cognitive Assessment score [0–30] — lower scores indicate cognitive impairment

26. FunctionalAssessment: Functional assessment score [0–10] — lower scores indicate greater impairment

27. Tremor: Presence of tremor [0: No, 1: Yes]

28. Rigidity: Presence of muscle rigidity [0: No, 1: Yes]

29. Bradykinesia: Presence of bradykinesia (slowness of movement) [0: No, 1: Yes]

30. PosturalInstability: Presence of postural instability [0: No, 1: Yes]

31. SpeechProblems: Presence of speech problems [0: No, 1: Yes]

32. SleepDisorders: Presence of sleep disorders [0: No, 1: Yes]

33. Constipation: Presence of constipation [0: No, 1: Yes]

34. Diagnosis: Parkinson's Disease diagnosis [0: No, 1: Yes]

35. DoctorInCharge: Confidential doctor identifier; the value is "DrXXXConfid" for all patients

Notes:
- PatientID is an identifier and should not be used as a predictive feature.
- DoctorInCharge is constant/confidential and should not be used as a predictive feature.
- The dataset is synthetic and was generated for educational purposes.
